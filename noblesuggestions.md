# Noble Rank Suggestions
**Theme: "Power Costs Freedom"** — Each rank brings wealth and power but chains you more tightly to one kingdom, one king, one fate.

---

## 👑 Minor Noble (Rank 4)

### Benefits
- **Court Whispers** — Receive advance notice of upcoming kingdom decisions (wars, trade bans, tax changes) 3-7 days before they happen. Huge trading advantage.
- **Noble Seal** — Your trade deals in your kingdom get a 5% price bonus (NPCs trust nobility). Stacks with reputation.
- **Tax Collector Rights** — King assigns you a small town; you collect 2% of its trade tax as passive income.
- **Noble Escort** — Free single guard when traveling within your own kingdom (no wages).

### Sacrifices
- **Royal Summons** — King periodically summons you to the capital (every 60-120 days). Must arrive within 14 days or lose 10 kingdom rep and a fine. Disrupts trading routes.
- **War Tithe** — When your kingdom goes to war, you must contribute 5% of your gold or 10% of your goods. Refusal costs 15 rep.
- **Appearances** — Must maintain a home worth at least 5,000g in your kingdom. If you don't, lose 1 rep/month. Nobles can't look poor.
- **Trade Restrictions** — Can no longer secretly trade with enemy kingdoms. Your noble seal makes you recognizable. Caught = treason, not just a fine.

---

## 🏰 Lord (Rank 5)

### Benefits
- **Town Steward** — King appoints you steward of a town. You set one local policy (tax rate ±5%, trade focus, security priority). Earn 5% of town's total tax revenue.
- **Land Grants** — King occasionally grants you free land in towns needing development. Build on it for profit.
- **Lord's Levy** — Can conscript 2-3 NPCs as temporary workers for free for 30 days (once per season). They work your buildings.
- **Trade Monopoly Petition** — Can petition king for exclusive rights to trade one resource in your steward town. If granted, all NPC merchants pay you a 3% cut.
- **Diplomatic Pouch** — Your caravans have 30% less encounter chance in your own kingdom (official business).

### Sacrifices
- **War Marshal** — During war, king demands you supply troops AND gold. 10% of gold + must send 2 of your guards to fight. They may not return.
- **Residency Requirement** — Must spend at least 60 days per year in your kingdom. Spending too long abroad costs 3 rep/month. You belong to the crown now.
- **Court Rivals** — Other lords actively scheme against you. Random events: rival lord sabotages a building (delays production 7 days), spreads rumors (-5 rep in a town), or poaches your workers.
- **King's Judgment** — King may order you to do something against your interests: shut down a profitable building, stop trading a resource, evict a worker. Refusal = 20 rep loss. Compliance = lost income.
- **Succession Target** — If the king dies, there's political chaos. You may be accused of involvement. 15% chance of temporary imprisonment during succession.

---

## 📜 Royal Advisor (Rank 6)

### Benefits
- **The King's Ear** — Propose laws directly (already exists), but now with much higher success rate. Can propose 1 law per season that the king almost always accepts.
- **Kingdom Treasury Access** — Can take interest-free loans from the kingdom treasury (up to 10% of treasury, repay within 1 year).
- **Royal Monopoly** — Get exclusive kingdom-wide trade rights for one luxury resource. All trade of that resource in the kingdom pays you a 5% royalty.
- **Crown Immunity** — Complete legal immunity in your own kingdom. Cannot be fined, jailed, or accused of any crime domestically.
- **Kingmaker** — If the king dies with no heir, YOU choose the new king (from eligible nobles). Install a puppet and control the kingdom from behind the scenes.

### Sacrifices
- **Tied to the Crown** — If your king is deposed in a war or revolt, you are exiled. Lose ALL rank, property, and 50% of gold in that kingdom. You fall back to Peasant. The higher you climb, the harder you fall.
- **No Other Masters** — Cannot hold rank above Citizen in any other kingdom. Must renounce higher ranks elsewhere upon becoming Royal Advisor. Your loyalty is singular.
- **Kingdom Financier** — King expects you to personally fund kingdom projects. Random demands: 5,000-25,000g for walls, roads, military. Refusal = king loses trust, stops accepting your proposals.
- **Assassination Risk** — 1-2% chance per season of assassination attempt by political rivals. Can be injured or killed. Hiring extra guards and high relationship with other nobles reduces risk.
- **The People's Resentment** — Common NPCs in your kingdom have -10 starting relationship with you. They see you as the elite. Makes it harder to get petition signatures, hire workers, or do business with regular townsfolk.
- **War Council** — You MUST attend war councils. During wartime, you cannot leave the capital for more than 7 days at a time. Your entire merchant operation runs without you.

---

**The overall arc:** As a peasant/merchant, you're free — trade anywhere, live anywhere, owe nothing. Each noble rank brings wealth and power but chains you more tightly to one kingdom, one king, one fate. A Royal Advisor is the richest and most powerful person in the realm... until their king falls.
